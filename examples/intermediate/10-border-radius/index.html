Border Radius

border-radius

border-radius: <length> | <percentage>

0

0 — square corners (default)

10px

10px — rounded

50%

50% — circle

10px 20px

10px 20px — two-value radii

10px 20px 30px

10px 20px 30px — three-value radii

10px 20px 30px 40px

10px 20px 30px 40px — all four corners

pill

50% on a wide box = pill

Global keywords also apply: inherit · initial (0) · revert · unset.

border-top-left-radius / border-top-right-radius

border-<corner>-radius: <length> | <percentage>

card top

top-left 20px, top-right 20px

50%

border-top-left-radius: 50%

ellipse

top-right 10px 20px — h/v radii

border-bottom-left-radius / border-bottom-right-radius

border-<corner>-radius: <length> | <percentage>

card bottom

bottom-left 20px, bottom-right 20px

50%

border-bottom-left-radius: 50%

pill

both bottom corners 50%

border-start-start-radius / border-end-end-radius

border-<start|end>-<start|end>-radius: <length> | <percentage>

Logical corners follow the writing direction — the same declarations flip between LTR and RTL.

LTR

start-start 16px, end-end 16px (LTR)

RTL

same rules with direction: rtl

corner-shape

corner-shape: round | squircle | bevel | scoop | notch

Applied with border-radius: 24px. Unsupported browsers fall back to round corners (Chrome 136+).

round

round — circular arcs (default)

squircle

squircle — superellipse

bevel

bevel — flat diagonal cut

scoop

scoop — inward scoop
