Form Pseudo-classes — Advanced

:dir()

:dir(ltr) | :dir(rtl)

Left-to-right text

:dir(ltr) — aligned left

Right-to-left text

:dir(rtl) — aligned right

input:dir(rtl) — input with right-to-left text

Global keywords also apply: inherit · initial · revert · unset.

:lang()

:lang(en) | :lang(en, fr, de)

"English" — curly quotes

:lang(en) — English text with en quotes

«French» — French quotes

:lang(fr) — French text with guillemets

العربية — right-to-left Arabic

:lang(ar) — direction rtl applied

„Deutsch" — German quotes

:lang(en, fr, de) — any of multiple languages matches
