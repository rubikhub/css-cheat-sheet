Opacity

opacity

opacity: <number>

The striped background shows through each box — lower opacity means more transparency.

1

1 — fully opaque (default)

0

0 — fully transparent (invisible)

0.5

0.5 — 50% transparent

0.25

0.25 — 75% transparent

0.75

0.75 — 25% transparent

hover me

hover — fades from 1 to 0.4

Hover the teal box to see a smooth fade to opacity 0.4.

Global keywords also apply: inherit · initial (1) · revert · unset.
