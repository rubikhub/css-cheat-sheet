Descendant Combinator

A B

Targets all B elements inside A at any depth.

direct p

nested p — any depth

span — not matched

div p — all p inside div

span inside .card
nested span too
span outside — not matched

.card span — all span inside .card

h2 in article

nested h2 too

h2 outside — not matched

article h2 — all h2 inside article

Child Combinator

A > B

Targets only direct child B elements of A.

  • direct li
  • direct li
    • nested li — not matched

ul > li — direct li children only

direct a nested a — not matched

.nav > a — direct a children only

direct p

nested p — not matched

div > p — direct p children only

Adjacent Sibling Combinator

A + B

Targets the first B element immediately after A.

Heading

first p after h2

second p — not matched

h2 + p — first p right after h2

header
first .content after .header
second .content — not matched

.header + .content — .content immediately after .header

img + figcaption — figcaption immediately after img

General Sibling Combinator

A ~ B

Targets all B elements that follow A.

p before h2 — not matched

Heading

p after h2

another p after h2

h2 ~ p — all p after h2

header
content after header
another content after header

.header ~ .content — all .content after .header

input ~ label — all label after input
